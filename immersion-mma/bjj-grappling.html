Two Paths to Mastery

Gi vs. No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu: What's the Difference?

All Immersion memberships include full unlimited access to both Gi and No-Gi classes.

TRADITIONAL GI

The Tactical Chess Match

Trained in the traditional kimono. Lapel and sleeve grips add friction, slowing down scrambles and demanding pure mechanical leverage. Ideal for building solid defensive technique.

  • Heavy friction limits speed and protects joints
  • Deep lapel chokes & sleeve controls
  • Teaches patience, defensive escapes & posture
MODERN NO-GI (ADCC STYLE)

Fast, Dynamic & Wrestling-Integrated

Trained in rashguards and shorts. With no cloth to grip, matches rely on underhooks, wrist control, explosive wrestling transitions, and modern leg lock entanglements.

  • Coached by Lee Ting (Pan-Pacific Champion)
  • Heel hooks, kneebars, and front headlock guillotine chains
  • Direct crossover to MMA cage grappling
Systematic Mat Pedagogy

The 3-Part Class Structure: How Every Class Runs

Our rotating beginner and intermediate curriculum ensures students can start anytime without feeling left behind. Two Black Belt coaches on the mat every evening provide continuous hands-on guidance.

PART 1 (15 MINS)

Dynamic Warm-Up & Mobility

Jiu-Jitsu specific joint mobility, hip escapes, forward and backward technical tumbling, and core activation designed to prepare muscles and prevent injuries.

PART 2 (30 MINS)

Technical Instruction & Drilling

Step-by-step mechanical breakdown from Head Coach Lee Ting and Sam Chew. Techniques are drilled repeatedly with cooperative resistance until muscle memory sets in.

PART 3 (15–20 MINS)

Positional Sparring & Rolling

Controlled, live resistance rolling. Beginners focus exclusively on positional survival and tap etiquette in our zero-ego, safety-first rolling culture.
