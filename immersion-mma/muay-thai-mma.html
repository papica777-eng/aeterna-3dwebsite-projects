Complete Striking Arsenal

The Mastered Weapons of Muay Thai

Authentic Muay Thai teaches you to utilize fists, elbows, knees, and shins for complete self-defense.

Punches (Chok)

Jabs, crosses, hooks, and overhands combined with angles and head movement.

Kicks (Te)

Rotational roundhouse kicks utilizing the shinbone to target legs, body, and head.

Knees (Khao)

Close-range power strikes driven from hip thrusts and clinch control.

Clinch (Chap Kho)

Stand-up control to off-balance opponents, neutralize strikes, and create openings.
