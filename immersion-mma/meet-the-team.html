Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Head Coaches & Black Belts

Lee Ting

Co-Founder & Head Coach · BJJ 3rd Degree Black Belt
Lineage: Machado BJJ Lineage

Lee Ting is an Immersion Co-Founder, Head BJJ Coach and a BJJ 3rd Degree Black Belt. After an intense 8-year journey to black belt under the Machado lineage, Lee became a Pan Pacific Champion and Boa Super 8 competitor. His teaching philosophy emphasizes pure body mechanics, calm defensive survival, and systematic Jiu Jitsu progression for practitioners of every age and physical attribute, in an ego-free training environment.

Credentials: Pan Pacific Champion · BOA Super 8 Competitor · 3rd Degree Black Belt · Machado Lineage

James Fitzgerald

BJJ Black Belt · Wrestling & MMA Coach
Awarded by Lee Ting (Joined 2013)

James Fitzgerald joined Immersion in 2013 and was awarded his BJJ Black Belt by Lee Ting. With a strong martial arts background including 15+ years of Taekwondo, wrestling, and MMA, James is also a practicing Myotherapist who blends anatomical precision, joint mobility, and explosive cage wrestling control.

Credentials: BJJ Black Belt · 15+ Yrs Taekwondo · Qualified Myotherapist · MMA & Wrestling Coach

Sam Chew

Senior Coach · BJJ Black Belt
Judo Background & Precision Submission Specialist

Blending foundational Judo throws with precision ground technique, Sam coaches morning and midday classes with meticulous technical clarity.

Credentials: IBJJF Worlds 3rd · Asian Open 2nd · Australian National Champion 1st · Pan Pac Champ · Judo

Nikki Lloyd-Griffiths
