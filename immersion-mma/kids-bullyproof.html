Our Two Age-Split Youth Programs

AGES 4–7 · LITTLE GRAPPLERS

Fun, Tumbling & Core Discipline

Through martial arts games, obstacle drills, and basic grappling holds, 4 to 7-year-olds develop body awareness, listening focus, and emotional self-regulation.

  • Listening to instructions on the first count
  • Safe falling & gymnastics agility
  • Standing up for themselves verbally with zero hitting
AGES 8–14 · YOUTH CHAMPIONS

Real BJJ, Striking Defence & Leadership

Older kids learn technical Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu control and kickboxing footwork. They build genuine physical resilience, mental calmness, and true anti-bullying capability.

  • Escape pins, headlocks, and ground attacks with ease
  • Earn authentic belt ranks and stripes
  • Screen detox: build real physical athletic ability
