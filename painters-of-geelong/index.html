    Are you a local painting company near me?

    Yes. Painters of Geelong is a local, owner-operated painting company providing residential and commercial painting services throughout Geelong and surrounding suburbs, including Belmont, Highton, Newtown, Grovedale, Torquay, Ocean Grove, Lara, Waurn Ponds and Armstrong Creek.     Do you offer free house painting quotes in Geelong?

    Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all residential and commercial painting projects across Geelong. Every quote is itemised, transparent and tailored to your property's needs, so you know exactly what's included before any work begins.

    How do I compare quotes from Geelong painters to get the best deal?

    Get at least three written painting quotes based on the same scope of work. Compare surface preparation, repairs, paint products, number of coats, exclusions, timelines, insurance and warranty terms alongside the total price. The cheapest quote may not provide the best value if important work is excluded. For more detailed guidance, read our interior painter quote comparison checklist or guide to choosing exterior painters in Geelong.

    What painting services do you offer in Geelong?

    We provide a complete range of residential and commercial painting services across Geelong, including interior house painting, exterior house painting, commercial painting, deck and timber staining, fence painting, and surface preparation and pressure cleaning.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes, 100%. Painters of Geelong is fully licensed (ABN 74 756 996 878, Est. 2012) and carries $20,000,000 in comprehensive Public Liability Insurance on every job. Furthermore, our workmanship is backed by a 10-Year Dulux Accredited Warranty on all eligible multi-coat systems.

    Do you provide Commercial, Strata, and after-hours painting in Geelong?

    Yes. We service commercial offices, retail shops, medical clinics, hospitality venues, and Strata body corporates across Greater Geelong. We provide flexible after-hours and weekend painting schedules so your normal business trading is never disrupted.     How much does house painting cost in Geelong?

    House painting costs vary depending on the size of your home, the condition of the surfaces, the type of paint selected and the scope of work. We provide a free, itemised quote for every job so you know exactly what to expect before committing — contact us today for a fast estimate.
