Terms of Trade & AS1288 Workmanship Warranty

Clear, fair Australian trade terms governing quotes, 24/7 emergency attendance, custom glass fabrication, and statutory warranties under ABN 27 564 832 883.

1. Obligation-Free Quotes & Scope Confirmation

Written and photo-based quotes issued by Original Property Group and Original Glazing (ABN 27 564 832 883) remain valid for 30 calendar days unless raw material or custom toughened/IGU furnace surcharges change prior to acceptance. Photo estimates are subject to final millimetre check-measure and verification of frame integrity on-site before cutting or ordering custom glass.

2. Australian Standard AS1288 Compliance

We strictly refuse to install non-compliant annealed glass in human-impact locations (doors, sidelights, bathrooms, low-level windows, or stairwells). All glazing installations are engineered in strict accordance with Australian Standard AS1288 (Glass in Buildings — Selection and Installation).

3. Custom Toughened Glass, Mirrors & Double Glazing Orders

Custom-manufactured 10mm toughened shower screens, Starphire splashbacks, custom mirrors, and hermetically sealed Insulated Glass Units (IGUs) are cut and furnace-toughened specifically to your property's millimetre measurements. Once custom production is authorized by the client (typically accompanied by a 50% material deposit), dimensions cannot be altered.

4. 24/7 Emergency Make-Safe & After-Hours Dispatch

Emergency callouts outside normal Melbourne business hours prioritize immediate hazard elimination—clearing dangerous shards and installing 6.38mm laminated safety glass from van stock where suitable, or installing weatherproof security board-ups pending custom glass manufacture.

5. 12-Month Workmanship Warranty & Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law (Competition and Consumer Act 2010). In addition to statutory rights, we warrant our installation workmanship and perimeter weather-sealing for 12 months from completion, alongside manufacturer warranties on sealed double-glazed units and hardware. Thermal stress fractures, impact damage after handover, or building structural movement are excluded.
