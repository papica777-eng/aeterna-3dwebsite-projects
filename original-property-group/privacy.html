Australian Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Entity: David Coluccio trading as Original Property Group and Original Glazing (ABN 27 564 832 883), Melbourne VIC, Australia.

1. Commitment to the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)

Original Property Group and Original Glazing (ABN 27 564 832 883) respect your privacy and manage all personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use, and protect personal data provided by residential homeowners, tenants, real estate agencies, owners corporations, NDIS plan managers, and commercial clients across Victoria.

2. Personal Information We Collect

  • Contact & Property Identification: Full name, company/agency name, mobile/landline number, email address, and Melbourne job-site street address or lot number.
  • Site Photographs & Scope Measurements: Window, glass, roof, plumbing, electrical, or structural repair photographs voluntarily submitted by you or recorded on-site strictly to prepare quotes, verify AS1288 compliance, or document completed work for landlords, strata committees, and insurers.
  • Concession & Billing Records: ABN details, work-order references, NDIS participant/plan manager billing instructions, or Pensioner Concession eligibility confirmation (we never store full credit card numbers on our website).

3. How & Why We Use Your Information

We use personal information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Dispatching 24/7 emergency glaziers and qualified Melbourne tradespeople to your property;
  • Preparing accurate obligation-free quotes, custom glass manufacturing schedules, and GST/ABN tax invoices;
  • Communicating arrival ETAs with tenants, building managers, or property owners;
  • Issuing AS1288 safety glass compliance documentation and warranty records.

We never sell, rent, or trade customer phone numbers, emails, or addresses to third-party lead brokers or marketing lists.

4. Disclosure to Trusted Trade & Regulatory Partners

Information is shared solely on a strict need-to-know basis with our licensed Melbourne trade personnel, custom Australian glass toughening/IGU fabricators (dimensions only), your nominated real estate property manager, insurer, or NDIS plan manager, or where required under Victorian building and safety law.
