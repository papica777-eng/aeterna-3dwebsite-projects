Our Story

From Melbourne's Trusted 24/7 Glaziers to Complete Property Care

Led by founder David Coluccio (ABN 27 564 832 883), our journey started with Original Glazing—answering urgent day-and-night calls across Melbourne to secure broken windows, upgrade dangerous float glass to Australian Standard AS1288, and install precision shower screens, mirrors, and double-glazed units.
